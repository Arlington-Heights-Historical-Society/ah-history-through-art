# Harmony Park Theme

![Alt text](https://d2fyd5kvehusob.cloudfront.net/images/histart/harmony/harmony-park-today.webp "Harmony Park Today")

The American house tavern was built by Peter and Mary Busch in the late 1800s and became a community staple. 

![Alt text](https://www.idaillinois.org/digital/api/singleitem/image/narlingto04/114/default.jpg)

[More info on Peter and Mary 
Busch](https://www.idaillinois.org/digital/collection/narlingto04/id/114/rec/18) from 1899. 

![Alt text](https://www.idaillinois.org/digital/iiif/narlingto04/3582/full/full/0/default.jpg)
The tavern changed owners a few times and in 
1917 it was renamed to the Vail. 

![Alt text](https://www.idaillinois.org/digital/iiif/narlingto04/2655/full/full/0/default.jpg)

It remained the vail until it burned down 
in 1988. 

![Alt text](https://d2fyd5kvehusob.cloudfront.net/images/histart/harmonypark/the-vail-tavern-fire.webp)

Many people mourned the passing of a beloved gathering 
place. 

![Alt text](https://www.idaillinois.org/digital/iiif/narlingto04/3456/full/full/0/default.jpg)

In 2005 Harmony Park was constructed on its lot. 

![Alt text](https://www.idaillinois.org/digital/iiif/narlingto04/3477/full/full/0/default.jpg)

Today the 
[sounds of summer](https://www.vah.com/explore/sounds_of_summer_concerts.php)
concert series take place in the park continuing the traditional of being a staple of the downtown area. [The 
location](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DTaVPmeUzkxobbs3A) downtown. 


**Artistic takes:**
* Maybe some paintings of Peter, Mary and others building or operating the 
American Tavern. Maybe a painting showing it as the center of the 
community.
* Depictions of the fire and the impact it had on the community
* interpretations of how the spirit lives on in Harmony park and summer 
concert series
* speculations on what it will look like in the future (50 years, 100?)
